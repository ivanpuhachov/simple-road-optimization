Route detail

Curve sample spacing is the target gap between sampled road vertices. Every vertex has a height that the solver can change. Smaller gaps give more detail and take more time.

Points that can be displaced sets how many interior vertices have independent sideways moves in the 2D search. Small dots mark them on the route. The solver spreads each move to the points between them. The two endpoints stay fixed.

Fewer move points and fewer search trials usually finish sooner. They can miss a better route. This setting does not change the 0.25 m grid used to estimate earthwork during optimization.
